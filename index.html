About Me

I am Abdulrauf Shaikh, a full-stack web developer and final-year ICT student at VGEC Ahmedabad with a CGPA of 8.39. Over the past two years, I have independently built multiple production-ready Django applications - from e-commerce platforms and job portals to blogging platforms with OAuth, dynamic theming engines, and Bloom Filter optimizations.
